🎬 THE DEMO JOURNEY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Visitors will experience:

Step 1: Ticket Inception (3s)
   → Alert arrives, AI picks up instantly

Step 2: AI Analysis (4s)
   → Reasoning traces, root cause, correlations

Step 3: Recommended Actions (5s)
   → 3 action cards with approve/reject

Step 4: Execution (4s)
   → Live progress: ticket updated, driver installed

Step 5: KB Article Draft (6s) ⭐ THE DIFFERENTIATOR
   → FULL KB article auto-generated
   → Shows title, issue, root cause, solution, prevention
   → This is what separates BuboIQ

Step 6: Ticket Closure (manual)
   → Summary: 6 min vs 45 min traditional
   → CTA: "Want this on your devices?"

Lead Capture → Success → Emails Sent
